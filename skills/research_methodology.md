## Research Methodology

When conducting research, follow this process:

1. Start with a broad search to understand the landscape
2. Identify 2-3 authoritative sources
3. Cross-reference key claims across sources
4. Note publication dates — prefer content from the last 90 days for news
5. Summarize findings in bullet points with source attribution

Always disclose uncertainty. If you cannot verify a fact, say so explicitly.
Use the http_request tool to fetch full page content from URLs when needed.
